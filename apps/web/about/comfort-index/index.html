Comfort index: how it is computed

The comfort index places an hour on the water between Comfortable and Discomfort for a type of boat, from forecast wind, waves, wind against tide and, for kayaks, the current and the cold. This page gives the formula in full and works one hour through it. The short version is under the comfort entry on the About page; the limitations apply: it is an indicative index derived from forecasts, not a determination of whether to go.
