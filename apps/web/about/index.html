LONGHORIZON.BLUE

About

longhorizon.blue shows public coastal-water observations and model forecasts, each with its age, source and recent history, so present conditions and the next two days read against recent weeks. The first waterway is Long Island Sound. Long Horizon Observatory curates the site as an early public demonstration, built from open data with open-source code.

Every plot and map layer has an (i) note: what it shows, the computation, the sources. The methods and sources pages collect those notes, plus data sources, processing and design principles.

Limitations and use

The observations on this site are provisional and may contain errors, gaps and delays. Model values are forecasts, not measurements. The comfort index and the wind-against-tide layer are indicative products derived from those data; their thresholds are judgments, not standards. The site is a demonstration, provided for general information, as is and without warranty of any kind.

It is not a navigation aid and is not intended for navigation, safety-of-life, operational or legal decisions. Consult the National Weather Service marine forecasts and warnings and NOAA's official charts; the decision to go out, and how, rests with the vessel's operator. Use of the site is subject to the terms.

Credits

  • Buoys and HF radar: LISICOS, Department of Marine Sciences, University of Connecticut, through the LISICOS ERDDAP server; HF radar totals from the NOAA IOOS HF Radar National Network, served by NOAA NDBC.
  • Shore stations, tides and currents: NOAA CO-OPS, and USGS tidal gauges.
  • Coastal airports: NWS ASOS, through the Iowa Environmental Mesonet.
  • Rivers: USGS Water Data for the Nation.
  • Forecasts and models: NOAA HRRR and GFS; the NWS Nearshore Wave Prediction System; NECOFS (FVCOM, UMass Dartmouth; NECOFS/UMASSD); Rutgers' DOPPIO (ROMS, for MARACOOS).
  • Satellite: the MUR sea surface temperature analysis (NASA JPL); VIIRS chlorophyll-a and water clarity through NOAA NESDIS CoastWatch.
  • Advisories: the National Weather Service New York office.

Every series names its source beside the values it supplies. The regional picture is on the NERACOOS Mariners' Dashboard.
