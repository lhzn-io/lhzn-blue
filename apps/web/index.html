LONGHORIZON .BLUE Alpha

Measurements from the water, made public: what each waterway reads now, how that compares with the same dates in earlier years, and where every number comes from. One page per waterway, each built with the people who care about it.

Long Island Sound Water temperature through the water column, with wind and air temperature, at four buoys from Execution Rocks to the Central Sound, set against the same dates in every earlier year on record, and the record we keep when a buoy goes quiet. Open

Data from LISICOS (University of Connecticut); regional context at the NERACOOS Mariners' Dashboard. Not for navigation.

By Long Horizon Observatory. Open source: github.com/lhzn-io/lhzn-blue.
