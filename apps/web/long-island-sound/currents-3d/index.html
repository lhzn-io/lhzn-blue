Long Island Sound from Throgs Neck to Block Island Sound: the NECOFS model's surface and bottom currents, and its full depth along the Sound's axis, hourly, from five days back to two days ahead.

Drag to turn the view, scroll or pinch to zoom. The layers button (top right) shows the curtains (along the Sound's axis; in the eastern Sound, across The Race or along the Thames) and, in the eastern Sound, every layer; the legend and the notes are at the bottom left.

The whole Sound shows the surface layer, the bottom layer's speed on the model's bed, and every layer only along its axis; the eastern Sound shows every layer. The model's mesh is coarser here than in its harbour grids: element sides of about 160 m in the Thames and at the Race's edges, 1 to 1.5 km in open water. The seabed is the model's own. The model's profile is drawn at the Groton current meter (NOAA CO-OPS nl0101).

Data: currents from the NECOFS FVCOM model (GOM7 mesh), University of Massachusetts Dartmouth (SMAST), from its server (NECOFS/UMASSD): its daily archive, then its rolling forecast.

Model output as published; not for navigation. See the terms for use and credit.
