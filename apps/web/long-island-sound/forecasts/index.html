The models at The Race

A model cannot show anything smaller than its cells: the Race's jet, a few kilometers wide, falls inside one or two cells of the global models and across dozens of NECOFS triangles. Shading: the water NECOFS models. Faint lines: its depths, every 10 m to 30 m, then 50 and 80 m. Under each map, on one axis to 7 days: the bar is how far ahead the site shows the model, each tick one of its time steps, and the line, for the wind models, the mean absolute error of wind speed against the buoys by lead, on one scale. The wind records cover different periods (HRDPS's only weeks, in autumn), so the lines compare in kind; a comparison over the same hours is to come. NECOFS is set beside HF radar and NOAA's New London current meter on the Sound's page; over October 3 to 20, 2026, it kept the tide's timing within about 10 minutes of NOAA's predictions at the Race, the central Sound and Throgs Neck. By buoy, season and speed: Forecast verification.

One 50 by 27 km patch in each model's own cells, by what it forecasts, finest first; under each, its reach in time and, for wind, its error.
