About this page

Models. NECOFS: FVCOM GOM7, SMAST; triangular mesh, elements 80 m to 37 km across. DOPPIO: ROMS, Rutgers, for MARACOOS; 7 km curvilinear grid. Each is shown on its own grid, not interpolated. Area: DOPPIO's water cells and the NECOFS elements centered in them.

Values. Each model's top layer (Surface), or a fixed depth below the surface, interpolated between the model's own layers; cells shallower than that depth are left out. Field: current speed, temperature (°C) or salinity (practical salinity), each element of NECOFS taking the mean of its three nodes. Arrow: current direction, fixed length. Gray lines: each model's own depth, 100 to 3000 m (50 m from zoom 7). Hover over a cell, or tap it, for its values on both maps. The depth maps and readouts follow 3DSeaVizKit's horizontal depth maps and hover values, kept here on each model's own grid rather than regridded.

Colors. Fixed scales: speed on the site's currents scale; temperature 0 to 30 °C and salinity 29 to 37 after cmocean's thermal and haline (Thyng et al. 2016). Show: color, arrows, grid (cell outlines) and depth lines, each on or off on both maps; with the color off, the two models' grids and depths compare alone.

Time. Latest forecast: every 2 hours, from 24 hours before the latest daily run to 72 hours after (DOPPIO: about 48 hours). Past days: hourly, by UTC day, up to 7 at a time; at depth from the first day the daily run archived them (2026-11-20), at the surface from each model's archive: NECOFS's hindcast and GOM7 archive, DOPPIO's reanalysis (to 2024) and forecast history. The address holds the period, hour and map area, so a copied link opens the same view.

Zoom. Below map zoom 7, NECOFS is the area-weighted mean of its elements in each DOPPIO cell; where its elements are larger than a cell (offshore), the elements themselves. From zoom 7, its own elements in view.

Model output, updated daily; not observations, not for navigation. The area overlaps the NES-LTER site; this page is not affiliated with it.

Data: NECOFS/UMASSD (SMAST); DOPPIO (Rutgers, MARACOOS). Citations: References. Basemap: OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap contributors.
