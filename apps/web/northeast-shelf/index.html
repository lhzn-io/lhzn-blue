NECOFS and DOPPIO, Cape Hatteras to Nova Scotia, 24 hours back to 72 hours ahead.

About this page

Models. NECOFS: FVCOM GOM7, SMAST; triangular mesh, elements 80 m to 37 km across. DOPPIO: ROMS, Rutgers, for MARACOOS; 7 km curvilinear grid. Each is shown on its own grid, not interpolated. Area: DOPPIO's water cells and the NECOFS elements centered in them.

Values. Top-layer velocity, every 2 hours from 24 hours before the latest daily run to 72 hours after (DOPPIO: about 48 hours). Color: speed. Arrow: direction, fixed length.

Zoom. Below map zoom 7, NECOFS is the area-weighted mean of its elements in each DOPPIO cell (offshore, where elements are larger, the element at the cell center). From zoom 7, its own elements in view.

Model output, updated daily; not observations, not for navigation. The area overlaps the NES-LTER site; this page is not affiliated with it.

Data: NECOFS/UMASSD (SMAST); DOPPIO (Rutgers, MARACOOS). Citations: References. Basemap: OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap contributors.
