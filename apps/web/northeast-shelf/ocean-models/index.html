About this page

Models. NECOFS: FVCOM GOM7, SMAST; triangular mesh, elements 80 m to 37 km across. DOPPIO: ROMS, Rutgers, for MARACOOS; 7 km curvilinear grid. Each is shown on its own grid, not interpolated. Area: DOPPIO's water cells and the NECOFS elements centered in them.

Values. Each model's top layer (Surface), or a fixed depth below the surface, interpolated between the model's own layers; cells shallower than that depth are left out. Field: current speed, temperature (°C) or salinity (practical salinity), each element of NECOFS taking the mean of its three nodes. Arrow: current direction, fixed length. Gray lines: each model's own depth, 100 to 3000 m (50 m from zoom 7). Hover over a cell, or tap it, for its values on both maps, its center, and the model's own depth there (averaged, like the values, in NECOFS's averaged view). The depth maps and readouts follow 3DSeaVizKit's horizontal depth maps and hover values, kept here on each model's own grid rather than regridded.

Colors. Fixed scales: speed on the site's currents scale; temperature 0 to 30 °C and salinity 29 to 37 after cmocean's thermal and haline (Thyng et al. 2016). Show: color, arrows, grid (cell outlines) and depth lines, each on or off on both maps; with the color off, the two models' grids and depths compare alone.

Time. Latest forecast: every 2 hours, from 24 hours before the latest daily run to 72 hours after (DOPPIO: about 48 hours). Past days: hourly, by UTC day, up to 7 at a time; at depth from the first day the daily run archived them (2026-11-20), at the surface from each model's archive: NECOFS's hindcast and GOM7 archive, DOPPIO's reanalysis (to 2024) and forecast history. The address holds the period, hour and map area, so a copied link opens the same view.

Measured. On some past days, the OOI Pioneer Array's moorings on the New England shelf break (2013 to 2022): gold rings filled by measured speed on the same scale, with a gold arrow for the direction. Each is a velocity profiler: hour means at the page's depths, interpolated between its bins and never beyond them, after dropping bins near the surface (where its echo off the surface spoils them), speeds over 2.5 m/s and bins with high error velocity. None reaches the models' top layer. Under each map: that model's vector RMS difference from the moorings over the day's hours at the chosen depth, from the same cells the map draws. Click a mooring for its time series under the maps: the current along the shelf (toward 260°) and across it (toward 350°, onshore), measured and both models, hourly over the days shown. Period menu: Pioneer moorings, Aug 2022.

Zoom. Below map zoom 7, NECOFS is the area-weighted mean of its elements in each DOPPIO cell; where its elements are larger than a cell (offshore), the elements themselves. From zoom 7, its own elements in view.

Model output, updated daily, with measured currents on some past days; not for navigation. The area overlaps the NES-LTER site; this page is not affiliated with it.

Data: NECOFS/UMASSD (SMAST); DOPPIO (Rutgers, MARACOOS). Measured currents: Ocean Observatories Initiative (OOI), Coastal Pioneer Array, funded by the U.S. National Science Foundation. Undersea names: Marine Regions (VLIZ, CC BY 4.0), from the US Board on Geographic Names (ACUF), the Canadian Geographical Names Database and the IHO-IOC GEBCO Gazetteer of Undersea Feature Names. Protected areas: NOAA Marine Protected Areas Inventory, a catalog, not the regulatory boundaries. Citations: References. Basemap: OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap contributors.
